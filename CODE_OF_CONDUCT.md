# Contributor Code Of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want this repository to be welcoming, practical, and respectful for everyone.
Contributors should be able to ask questions, report issues, and discuss ideas
without being attacked or belittled.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Focus feedback on the code, behavior, or proposal rather than on the person.
- Share reproduction steps, logs, and evidence when reporting problems.
- Assume good intent first, then clarify when something is unclear.
- Keep discussions useful for future readers.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Personal attacks, harassment, or threats.
- Discriminatory language or exclusionary behavior.
- Trolling, baiting, or intentionally derailing technical discussion.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated low-signal or abusive issue / PR spam.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that makes collaboration
unsafe or unproductive. Repeated or severe violations may lead to temporary or
permanent bans from the project spaces.

## Reporting

If you need to report a conduct issue, open a private report if that channel is
available, or contact the maintainers through the repository owner account
instead of posting public accusations.
